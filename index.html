Tabdeel // SectionSwap Engine

Autonomous Peer-to-Peer Academic Schedule Reconciler

University section registrations frequently devolve into chaotic WhatsApp spreadsheets where hundreds of students fail to find mutual swaps. Tabdeel is an autonomous bilateral and multi-hop cycle matching engine. It ingests enrolled student sections (A1–D7) alongside desired target schedules, executing instant constraint satisfaction and generating automated direct contact pairings without human bottlenecks.

Invention 02 Physical Computing Hardware Prototype

ChronoGrid Horizon

Tactile Ambient Focus Station & System Telemetry Display

Modern computer setups overflow with notification badges and browser tabs that shatter deep focus. ChronoGrid Horizon is an open-architecture desktop hardware device powered by an ESP32 microcontroller, dual magnetic rotary encoders, and a custom diffused matrix display. It pairs tactile Pomodoro intervals with silent ambient luminescence and low-latency system telemetry (CPU temperature, build status, network ping) — keeping you locked in flow without glancing at your phone.

Hardware Core
ESP32-S3 + Custom LED Diffuse Grid
Controls
Magnetic kinetic rotary dial
Telemetry
WiFi UDP System Ping & Ambient Temp
Aesthetic
Matte Stealth Slate & Sandblasted Acrylic

Born from breadboard DIY electronics & desk setup optimization research.

FlowCommerce // The "Fragile" Engine

High-Conversion Frictionless Storefront for Social Brands

Independent lifestyle brands and modern makers face a harsh dilemma: heavy Shopify stores with high drop-off rates, or messy Instagram DMs with manual order chaos and high Cash-on-Delivery (COD) cancellations. FlowCommerce is a sub-15KB headless catalog that converts browsers via instant WhatsApp structured order dispatch and an automated 50 EGP InstaPay deposit buffer script — preserving customer trust while eliminating rejected shipment losses.

Payload Weight
< 15 KB (Zero framework bloat)
Payment Rail

Core Operating Rules

  • 01. Trend Lag Arbitrage: Global technical and aesthetic movements take time to propagate locally. The real value lies in early identification, structural validation, and flawless execution.
  • 02. Unit Economics First: A clever idea with poor unit economics is an illusion. Every project must balance customer acquisition, return friction, and capital conversion.
  • 03. Physical Tactility: As the world moves purely digital, tangible objects on the desk—mechanical keys, kinetic gadgets, ambient illumination—anchor the human mind.
